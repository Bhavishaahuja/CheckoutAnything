Quill & Pipkin Studio

Character illustration by June. Commissions open

Hello!

I draw original characters, pets, and tabletop party portraits in a soft painterly style. Everything is digital and you get a high resolution file when it's done, usually within two weeks.

What it costs

Headshots are $40, half body $65, full body $90. Add $15 for a background. Extra character +50%.

Prices are for personal use. If you want to use the art commercially, write to me first and we'll work something out.

How to order

Send me a message with your references and which kind of piece you want. I'll send you an invoice, and I start once it's paid. There's nothing to ship, files are delivered by email.

What I won't draw

Mecha, real people, and anything you'd be embarrassed to show your grandmother.

Quill & Pipkin Studio is a fictional studio made for a demo.
