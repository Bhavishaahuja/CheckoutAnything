Demo stores for Checkout Anything

Three fictional stores with no checkout and no PayPal integration. Each one is messier than the last. Paste any of these URLs into Checkout Anything to make the store agent-ready.

  • Fernwood & Thistle Goods (a clean product grid)
  • Quill & Pipkin Studio (an illustrator's commission page, written as prose)
  • Hollowbrook Bakery (a menu with a sold out item, a crossed-out price and a footnote price)

These businesses are made up. Nothing here can really be ordered.
